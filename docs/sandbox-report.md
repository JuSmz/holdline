Observations:

- Payout errors carry the reason in the error **`name`** (e.g. `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS`), not in `details[0].issue` — classify payout failures by `name`.
- The send response's payer-view href (`…/invoice/p/#INV2-…`) and `detail.metadata.recipient_view_url` (`…#6VCV9CAE8B8PR7QJ`) differ in format; both open the same invoice.

## Other observations

- `POST /v2/checkout/orders` with `payment_source.paypal.experience_context` returns **HTTP 200** and order status **`PAYER_ACTION_REQUIRED`** (not 201/`CREATED`). Stage 6 treats `PAYER_ACTION_REQUIRED` as "awaiting approval".
- The GET of a `CAPTURED` authorization still lists `capture`/`void`/`reauthorize` HATEOAS links — state comes from `status` only.
- After a decline the order stays **approved** (`CHECKOUT.ORDER.APPROVED` arrived for the declined order `3L936651FS166391U`) at PayPal. Stage 6: the tab-closed recovery (`CHECKOUT.ORDER.APPROVED` webhook / reconciler) must NOT re-authorize an order whose last authorize ended in a definite decline; the payer has to approve again.
- Order/authorize/capture round trips took 0.5–1.7 s from Render (oregon).
